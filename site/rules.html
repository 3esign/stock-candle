PUBLIC RULES · PRE-LAUNCH

The ladder
only goes up.

Start with SOL or TSLAx and buy $XCNDL. If the verified buy crosses the next locked 1,000,000-token level, your wallet earns one rung. Most rungs after 15 minutes wins the SOL pot and TSLAx fee prize.

Enter

With SOL: request a fresh Jupiter ExactIn route, inspect its minimum TSLAx output and price impact, simulate, then approve the wallet transaction. Your wallet receives TSLAx.

With TSLAx: skip conversion and build the game transaction directly. No builder holds funds or player keys.

Climb

  • The game instruction calls Pump `buy_v2` and records state only after the real buy succeeds.
  • Observed player and curve token deltas must match the requested $XCNDL buy.
  • One transaction can earn at most one rung.
  • Sells never lower the high-water mark. Old rungs cannot be reclaimed.
  • If scores tie, the wallet that reached that score first remains ahead.

Settle

After 15 minutes, anyone may invoke settlement. The full stored lamport pot is paid once to the on-chain leader. The same winner can then permissionlessly claim the entire TSLAx balance held by the pot token account. If creator-fee shares arrive later, another claim sends them to that already-recorded winner. Failed or empty claims roll back.

Frozen launch rules

  • Window: 15 minutes from on-chain initialization.
  • Minimum buy: 1,000,000 XCNDL.
  • Candle step: 1,000,000 XCNDL.
  • Successful-rung fee: 0.001 SOL, added to the SOL pot.
  • Initial SOL pot: 0.05 SOL.
  • Tie: the wallet that reached the tied score first remains ahead.
  • Creator fee sharing: 66.33% to the game pot PDA and 33.67% to the creator wallet, locked through Pump fee sharing at launch.

Risk and identity

$XCNDL is a game token paired with TSLAx. It is not Tesla stock or equity, and STOCK CANDLE is not affiliated with Tesla or the TSLAx issuer. TSLAx access is jurisdiction-dependent. No reward, profit, price, liquidity or route is guaranteed.
