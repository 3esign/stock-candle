RULES THAT SURVIVE THE WEBSITE

The ladder only goes up.

What the program proves

A rung is recorded only after a real Pump buy_v2 succeeds and observed player and curve token deltas match the requested $XCNDL buy. A failed buy rolls everything back.

What cannot be recycled

One transaction earns at most one rung. Selling never lowers the stored high-water mark, so selling and buying again cannot reclaim an old level.

What the interface cannot own

The site and route builder prepare unsigned data. They hold no player funds or keys. The deployed program, token accounts and settlement remain callable without this website.
