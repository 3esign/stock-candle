# STOCK CANDLE - Design Contract

## Artifact Sentence

A public stock chart becomes a finite ladder: every verified TSLAx-paired buy physically adds one step, and Solana names the climber.

## What Will Not Be On Screen

No Tesla logo or car, fake live price, invented pot value, simulated wallet presented as real, admin control, casino decoration, atmospheric token art, nested card wall, or enabled trade button before deployment.

## Experience Thesis

Within five seconds a mobile visitor should understand what to buy, what it is paired with, how one rung is won, and how the pot settles.

## Mode And Signature

- Mode: generative Canvas 2D chart supported by a precise on-chain instrument panel.
- Signature gesture: the candlestick chart is the ladder. Wallet markers climb awarded green candles; the yellow next target never falls when sells occur.
- Meaning belongs to semantic HTML. Canvas is visual reinforcement and has an adjacent text summary.
- Reduced-motion mode freezes animation into a complete static state.

## Composition

1. First viewport: name, token/pair, one-sentence mechanic, entry mode, pre-launch status and the ladder chart.
2. Entry instrument: SOL or TSLAx selector, amount, wallet, fresh quote, simulation and explicit signature.
3. Game state: remaining time, next rung, leaders and pot, all read from chain or visibly pending.
4. Rules and trust boundary: exact settlement logic, replaceable route builder and immutable program evidence.
5. Social and risk footer: X, Telegram, tokenized-equity distinction and jurisdiction notice.

## Visual System

- Warm white field, black structure and grid.
- Green up candles, red sells, cobalt TSLAx and yellow next rung.
- Tight market typography, zero negative tracking, 8px maximum radius.
- Stable chart and control dimensions across 360px mobile through wide desktop.
